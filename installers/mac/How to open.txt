Bookshelf - a book reader for Mac

1. Drag Bookshelf into the Applications folder (in this window).
2. Open Applications and double-click Bookshelf.

The first time, the Mac says it "cannot check Bookshelf for malicious software"
(it says that for any app not registered with Apple):
  - Click "Done" (or "OK").
  - Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click "Open Anyway"
    next to the message about Bookshelf. Confirm with your password.
It only asks once.

Your library, reading progress and highlights are saved in
~/Library/Application Support/Bookshelf. Your book files are never changed.
